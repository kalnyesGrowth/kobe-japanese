Our Story

A Stafford Staple Since Day One

Kobe Japanese Steak and Seafood House has been a cornerstone of Stafford, offering authentic Japanese cuisine that celebrates the artistry of teppanyaki and the precision of fresh sushi. For over two decades, families and friends have gathered around our hibachi tables to witness culinary performances that turn dinner into an experience.

Our chefs bring 20+ years of expertise to every table, combining theatrical flair with serious culinary skill. Every cut of beef is 100% USDA certified, and our fish is delivered fresh, never frozen.
