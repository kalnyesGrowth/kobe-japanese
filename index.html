Our Story

A Stafford Tradition

Kobe Japanese Steak and Seafood House has been a cornerstone of Stafford for over two decades. Families and friends have gathered around our hibachi tables to witness culinary performances that turn dinner into an experience you won't forget.

Our chefs bring years of expertise to every table, combining theatrical flair with serious culinary skill. Fresh fish is delivered daily, never frozen. And every hibachi entree comes with soup, salad, shrimp appetizer, vegetables, fried rice, and tea.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a reservation for hibachi?
Reservations are strongly recommended for hibachi seating, especially on weekends. Each hibachi table has a dedicated chef with limited time slots. Walk-ins are welcome at the sushi bar and for regular dining. Call (540) 720-1935 to book.
What comes with a hibachi entree?
Every hibachi entree includes onion soup, house salad with ginger dressing, a shrimp appetizer, hibachi vegetables, fried rice, and hot tea. It's a complete multi-course meal.
Is Kobe good for families with children?
Absolutely. Many families with kids enjoy the hibachi experience because the cooking show keeps children entertained. We have a kids menu available and our staff is great with young guests.
Do you offer takeout or delivery?
Yes, we offer both takeout and delivery. Sushi rolls, hibachi entrees, and appetizers are all available to go. Call (540) 720-1935 to place your order.
Is the fish fresh?
Yes. Our fish is delivered fresh daily and is never frozen. This applies to all sushi, sashimi, and seafood dishes on our menu.
Do you have lunch specials?
Yes, lunch specials are available Wednesday through Friday from 11:30 AM to 2:30 PM. The lunch menu features smaller portions at reduced prices across hibachi and sushi options.
Can you accommodate large groups?
Yes, we are great for large groups and parties. Each hibachi table seats up to 8-10 guests. For larger events, call ahead so we can arrange the best seating for your group.
Do you serve alcohol?
We have a full bar with beer, wine, sake, and cocktails. Japanese-inspired cocktails and premium sake pair well with our dishes.
